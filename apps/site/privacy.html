What we collect

  • Your account. Your name, email address and the organisation you belong to. If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture, and nothing else from your Google account.
  • What you make. Places, vehicles, controllers and missions you upload or build, and the dives you run, with their recordings.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell your data.

Why

To run your account and your dives, to keep the service secure, and to answer you when you write to us.

Where it is kept

How long

For as long as your account is open. When you close it we delete your account and your organisation's data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.
