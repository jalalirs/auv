# Lab 00 — ROS 2 re-entry

## Objective

Rebuild the mental model of ROS 2 by observing a small graph rather than
memorizing commands.

## Topics

- nodes, topics, services, and actions;
- parameters and launch files;
- QoS and discovery;
- ROS time versus wall time;
- rosbag2 and MCAP; and
- graph inspection in Foxglove.

## Completion check

Explain the graph, change a parameter, inspect topic frequency and bandwidth,
record the graph, and replay it without the original publishers.
