# The eighth plan: the places module

*Proposed 6 October 2026.* A twin is only as real as its seabed. Today a place is made by about fifteen command-line tools run by hand in the right order: `get-bathymetry`, `get-reef`, `coral-atlas`, `icesat`, `fit-depths`, `fit-colour-depths`, `make-site`, `reef-survey`, `benthos`, `ground`, `surroundings`, `fauna`, `aim-water`, `check-depths`, `check-places`, with `built.py` and `rebuild` remembering how. They work, and six places came out of them, but each knows only its own source, and nothing combines two sources for the same square metre.

r8 turns them into one module: **places**. It takes whatever data a site has and builds the place from it. AI comes in where it earns its place, behind the same interfaces as everything else.

## What it is

```
sources  →  layers  →  fusion  →  place  →  scene
```

- **Sources** are adapters, one per kind of data. Each fetches or reads its data and hands back layers: Seabed 2030 (GEBCO and its type grid), the DCDB multibeam mosaic, ICESat-2 ATL24 depths, Sentinel-2 imagery, the Allen Coral Atlas, USGS and NOAA DEMs, iNaturalist and OBIS records, and a client's own GeoTIFF, XYZ points, multibeam grid or photogrammetry orthomosaic.
- **Layers** are one shape for everything: a grid on the site's frame with, cell by cell, a value, an error, and the source it came from. Depth, coral cover, habitat class, colony positions, colour.
- **Fusion** decides, cell by cell, which source to believe: the one with the smallest error where they overlap, blended at the seams, and recorded per cell. Models live here too: today's colour-to-depth fit, tomorrow a learned one, each a model that turns some layers into another layer with an error that was checked on held-out data.
- **The place** is the fused layers plus what grows on them: colonies from a survey where there is one, from zonation where there is not, and the fish recorded there.
- **The scene** is what a dive opens: `seabed.f32`, `seabed.usda`, `coral.usda`, the textures, and `site.json`, with every number's provenance.

A place is described by a **recipe**: the site (centre, size, cell), the sources to use, and the models to run. `places build <recipe>` makes it; the recipe is kept with the place, so rebuilding is running it again.

## Build order

1. **The structure.** The `places` package: `Layer`, the source interface, fusion, the recipe, and the scene writer taken out of `make-site`. The existing tools become sources and models inside it, one at a time, each checked by rebuilding a place it built before and comparing.
2. **Sources we already have,** moved in: GEBCO and DCDB (from `surroundings`), ICESat-2 (`icesat`), Sentinel-2 (`get-reef`), the Allen Coral Atlas (`coral-atlas`), USGS surveys (`reef-survey`, `benthos`), fish records (`fauna`).
3. **Fusion with provenance per cell,** and a map of where the place is weak: which cells are measured, which derived, with what error.
4. **Client data in:** GeoTIFF, XYZ, multibeam grids, an orthomosaic.
5. **Models:** today's depth fits as the first; then a learned depth model trained across sites against ICESat-2 and multibeam; then coral cover from imagery with a confidence.
6. **A Place Builder tab:** point at data, watch the place build, see where it is weak.

## Progress

- *6 October 2026.* Steps 1 and 2 for depth: every place has a recipe in `packages/places/recipes/`, and each rebuilds its published seabed (table in `packages/places/README.md`). Found on the way and fixed: Al Fahal's fit had lifted 32 ha of reef flat dry (republished v7); get-bathymetry's NOAA fetches were stretched north to south, which had squeezed Looe Key's mosaic 9.3% (republished v10). The fetching moved in the same day: each source fetches into the reference folder when its files are missing, and Shushah built from an empty folder with one command matches its published seabed to within 1e-6 m. On the way, get-reef stopped picking scenes that do not cover the square.

- *6 October 2026, later.* Step 3: every cell carries its source and its error, a model's being its held-out error at that cell's depth; each place's record says where it is weak (`from.depth.weak`), carries the per-cell files and `weak.png` (`perCell`), and the MCP reports it with the error where a dive begins. Shushah, the Red Sea place, Kaneohe, Al Fahal and Looe Key rebuilt through the module and republished with it. Al Fahal, where most dives are flown, is the weak one: median 2.17 m, 36% of its seabed may be 5 m or more out, 3.27 m at its dive start.

- *7 October 2026.* Step 4: a client's raster (any coordinate system, averaged onto the grid), orthomosaic and XYZ. Looe Key built from satellite data alone and then with the SQUID-5 DEM laid over it as a client's survey: the survey replaces the satellite where it covers, 0.48 m rms from the real seabed against 3.77 m, and its edge is a slope within the real seabed's own. Found: the satellite seabeds are terraced in 10 m steps, because Sentinel-2's pixels are read by nearest neighbour; next.

- *7 October 2026, later.* The terracing fixed: get-reef reads Sentinel-2 bilinearly and is pinned to the scene a place was built from, the median colour is read bilinearly, and the atlas classes are softened where the colour fit draws (not where it fits: fitted softened, a class with no photons took a coefficient of hundreds of metres). At satellite-only Looe Key against its surveyed seabed: false steps 53.6 to 7.6 per 1000, rms 3.77 to 3.68 m. Shushah (v3) and Al Fahal (v9) republished: steps over half a metre between neighbours halved and a third fewer, held-out errors unchanged; Shushah's coastline redrawn from a smooth near infrared moved its dive start 150 m.

- *7 October 2026, evening.* Step 5 began as its own project, `ml/depth` (configs, dataset build, U-Net, evaluation, a container on the box's second GPU). depth-v1: 3,267 chips of Sentinel-2 over NOAA reef lidar in six regions, 35 M measured cells. unet-v1, tested on regions it never saw: 1.33 m in Florida (the per-pixel linear fit, 5.58 m), 1.58 m in the Virgin Islands, 3.56 m in Guam and the Marianas, 2.1 to 2.4 m on the Red Sea's ICESat-2 tracks. Samoa's surveys turned out to be in ellipsoid heights and are excluded. Next: the model into the places module as a source, with each place's ICESat-2 photons correcting it locally.

## Done when

- Each of the six places rebuilds from its recipe through the module, matching what the tools built.
- A place can be built for a new site from public data with one command, and says per cell where its depth came from and how wrong it may be.
- A client's GeoTIFF of a site replaces the satellite depths where it covers, and the seams are invisible in a dive.
