# The forum deck: flow

*6 October 2026.* The deck (forum-deck/index.html, not in git: films and a pitch) follows the same story as the second promo film (tools/promo/timeline2.html). Speaker notes are in the deck itself; press N, or P for a presenter window.

1. **iOcean** — a unified digital twin for marine operations, over the Al Fahal reef.
2. **The problem** — marine work is planned on paper and learned at sea; nobody sees the seabed, the current or the plume until the job is under way.
3. **And the sea pays for what nobody saw** — over a generated dredge-plume clip.
4. **What iOcean is** — one digital twin of the sea: place, assets, operations, each acting on the others.
5. **How it is built** — coupled physics; real seabeds and their sources; a source for every number (Shushah ±1.28 m, held out).
6. **The app** — planned at your desk, simulated on GPU hosts; the app, the API, AI agents through MCP.
7. **iOcean Robotics, phase one** — its parts, built (places, vehicles, controllers, missions, sweeps, remote runs, results) and next (Vehicle Lab, AI places, autonomy, synthetic data).
8. **Built: places** — Shushah.
9. **Built: vehicles** — the six packages.
10. **One job, end to end: plan** — lay the pipeline; plan it against the REMUS.
11. **One job: what could go differently** — four currents, flown remotely.
12. **One job: the result** — 452 of 452 m, 3 of 3 spans, nothing struck.
13. **This job's numbers** — derived, chosen, measured, assumed.
14. **Next in phase one** — what we are building now.
15. **Your vehicle, your controller, compared** — the SDK; the two-controller split.
16. **Roadmap** — Robotics → Ports · Energy · Reef → one ocean twin, with picture tiles.
17. **Who it is for.**
18. **The ask** — rehearse one of your jobs with us.
