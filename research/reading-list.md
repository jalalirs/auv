- Marine coordinate frames and navigation conventions

- Gazebo hydrodynamics and marine simulation
- Stonefish architecture and sensor models
- OpenUSD composition, variants, geospatial framing, and asset provenance
- Isaac Sim extensions, ROS 2 bridge, Replicator, and remote deployment

- Environmental digital-twin architecture and uncertainty communication
- In-situ sensor networks, Spotter/Smart Mooring telemetry, and data governance
- Coral heat stress, turbidity, light, oxygen, chemistry, and health indicators
- Site-scale geodesy, bathymetry, vertical datums, and local ENU frames
